A host that governs organizations can close every workspace and organization route its people do not need: with `OrganizationGovernancePort.memberRouteAccess: "allowlist"`, a person of a governed organization (their own browser session, native app credential or personal API key) reaches `/v1/workspaces/:workspaceId/*` and `/v1/organizations/:organizationId/*` only through the governed allowlist, and every other route answers `403` with `details.code = "WORKSPACE_ROUTE_NOT_ALLOWED"` before its own checks. The allowlist covers the workspace read, session list and create, the session routes whose operation is in `GOVERNED_MEMBER_SESSION_OPERATIONS`, the composer's reads, the workspace live streams, the session-bound first-party MCP endpoint, the inbox and notifications, documents, Knowledge, Skills, session feedback and artifact reads; no organization route. Tokens signed with the delegation secret and machine credentials are unaffected. New exports: `WORKSPACE_ROUTE_NOT_ALLOWED` (`@opengeni/contracts/organization-governance`); `GOVERNED_MEMBER_SESSION_OPERATIONS`, `GOVERNED_MEMBER_WORKSPACE_ROUTES`, `GOVERNED_MEMBER_ORGANIZATION_ROUTES`, `governedMemberRouteAllowed` and `governedRouteTable` (`@opengeni/api-router/app`), the last giving the decision for every mounted route so a host can generate its refusal tests. Without the opt-in nothing changes.
